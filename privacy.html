This policy explains how VML Hub Studio Payments handles information when processing studio purchases and delivering access keys. It covers the payment and email-delivery service; individual creative studios may have additional data practices.

Information we collect

We store information needed to process and support your purchase, including:

  • Your name and delivery email address.
  • The purchased product, payment amount, and payment status.
  • PayPal order and transaction references.
  • Your issued access key.
  • Purchase-confirmation emails, delivery status, and technical error information.
  • Session information and technical logs needed to operate and secure checkout.

PayPal processes payments. Our checkout does not collect or store your full payment-card number or PayPal password.

Google account access

Only an authorized VML Hub service administrator connects the sender’s Google account.

The integration accesses the connected account’s email address to verify the sender and requests permission to send purchase confirmations and access-key emails through Gmail.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored by the service so it can send messages without requiring the administrator to sign in for every email.

This integration does not request permission to read customers’ Gmail inboxes, contacts, or Google Drive files. Customers do not need to authorize Google access to make a purchase.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Verify payments and issue the correct studio access key.
  • Send purchase confirmations and activation instructions.
  • Maintain access-key eligibility.
  • Investigate delivery failures and respond to support requests.
  • Maintain payment records and address disputes or suspected misuse.

Information obtained through the Gmail integration is not used for advertising or training general-purpose AI models.

Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by services necessary to operate checkout and email delivery:

  • PayPal processes payments and provides transaction confirmations.
  • Google Gmail processes outgoing purchase emails and their contents.
  • Hugging Face hosts the checkout application and its persistent storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages and may process technical request information to serve them. These static pages do not collect purchase details or Google OAuth tokens.

Authorized administrators may review purchase and delivery records when providing support or maintaining the service. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Purchase records, access keys, retained outgoing messages, and Google OAuth credentials are stored in our service database and backups.

Administrative access is restricted, and connections to Google and PayPal use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain purchase records, access keys, and delivery records to provide ongoing access, support customers, and maintain necessary payment records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email vmlhub.ai@gmail.com. We may need to verify your ownership of the purchase. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, security, or dispute handling. Backup copies may remain until those backups are rotated or removed.

Revoking Google access

The connected sender-account owner can revoke the application’s access through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

Revocation prevents future authorized Gmail activity. It does not automatically delete existing purchase records or previously sent emails. The administrator can contact us to request removal of stored connection information.

Google API Limited Use

We follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
