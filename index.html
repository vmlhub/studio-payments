VML Hub Studio Payments handles PayPal purchases and access-key delivery for our AI video creation studios. Pay through PayPal, receive your key by email, and unlock the studio you purchased.

Choose your studio →How we handle your information

From checkout to creating

  1. Choose your studio

    Enter your name and delivery email. Current pricing is shown on the studio’s checkout page.

  2. Pay securely with PayPal

    Review and confirm your payment on PayPal. Our checkout does not collect your PayPal password or full payment-card number.

  3. Receive and activate your key

    After payment is confirmed, we issue a key and email it to the address you provided. Enter it in the matching studio and select Unlock.

Email delivery

Why we use Gmail

An authorized VML Hub administrator connects our sender account using Google OAuth. The Gmail API sends transactional purchase confirmations, access keys, and activation instructions after a confirmed payment.

The integration requests gmail.send to send these emails and userinfo.email to verify the connected sender’s email address.

Customers do not need to connect their Google accounts or grant access to their inboxes.

An access key unlocks application features. Hugging Face GPU usage limits remain applicable.

Google API use and responsible creation

The payment and email integration does not generate images or videos. Its Google API access is limited to verifying the administrator’s sender address and sending purchase emails. Studio prompts, uploaded media, and generated images or videos are not sent to Google through this integration. It does not use Google APIs to create, process, store, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII).

Permitted studio use

Use the studios only with content you own or have permission to use, and respect the privacy and rights of others. Creating, sharing, or facilitating non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated sexual images or videos of someone without their consent, is prohibited. Sexual content involving minors and other unlawful or rights-infringing uses are also prohibited.

To report misuse involving our service, contact vmlhub.ai@gmail.com. Describe the issue and provide a relevant reference or page URL; do not email intimate images or videos.
